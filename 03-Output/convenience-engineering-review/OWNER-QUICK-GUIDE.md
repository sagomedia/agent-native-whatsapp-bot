# Shop owner quick guide

- **Requests → Active:** follow the **Next** instruction. Checkout lists remain separate until the customer sends the order.
- **Missing prices/details:** review all items, send the final shop bill and wait for customer approval. The previous estimate stays in the history.
- **Accept & reserve stock:** check the shelf first. This reserves known stock without recording payment.
- **Pack:** check the exact products, quantities and sizes, tick the packing check, then mark ready. A customer-approved change requires another packing check.
- **Collected:** record this after handing over the items. It deducts reserved stock once.
- **Cancel:** releases the reservation. Any advance refund is recorded separately.
- **Earlier order:** no stock was changed during the upgrade. Use **Reconcile & reserve** only after checking that order physically.
- **Unknown/manual item:** check it physically. Unknown stock is never treated as zero or an available promise.
- **Products → Shop stock:** record received stock, a walk-in sale or the corrected physical count. Include a reason/receipt. Physical count includes reserved items.
- **Message review:** inspect current replies in Tamil, Tanglish or English. Preview controls are disabled; the sample QR cannot receive real payments.
- **₹10 advance:** check the actual bank/UPI receipt before verifying. The demo pay-at-shop choice records no advance.

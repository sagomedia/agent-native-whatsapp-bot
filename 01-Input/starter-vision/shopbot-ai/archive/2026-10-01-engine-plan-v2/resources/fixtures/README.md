# Engine fixture design

Seed two isolated synthetic retail stores: hardware and another profile such as packaged food. Include similar variants, different units, known/unknown/stale stock, and explicit demo prices.

Supply scripted mock text/image/audio outputs for repeatable state/permission checks. Mock inference must be visibly labeled and must not claim real language/image accuracy.

Include owner voice-change proposals, customer photos, owner product drafts, duplicate ingress, stale versions, budget exhaustion, and payment captured/failed/duplicate/out-of-order events.

The real hardware trial later replaces or separately imports 50 merchant-approved SKUs and held-out photos/voice notes. Do not overwrite real data with demo seeds.

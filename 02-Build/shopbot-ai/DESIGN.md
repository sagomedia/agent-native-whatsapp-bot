# Local simulator design

Purpose: let the developer inspect a hardware shopping draft and persistent conversation before live channels exist.

Layout: left catalog, central conversation, right draft; stack the draft and then all panels at smaller widths. Keep the catalog and draft visible together while testing known-item actions. All content is left aligned.

Palette: cool workbench background #EDF2F5, white panels #FFFFFF, dark blue text #192C3C, tool blue action #215D80, pale teal customer message #DCEEF1, yellow demo notice #FFF5CF.

Type: system Segoe UI/Tahoma for readable local tooling, 32px identity, 20px panel titles, 14–15px content. Product names, units and quantities carry the hierarchy. No promotional hero, decorative gradients, animation or stock photography.

The prominent demo notice makes provenance clear. Keyboard focus and responsive columns are required. Buttons name their actual effects. Draft totals use backend facts; checkout is absent until implemented. This is a development simulator, not the owner dashboard or customer WhatsApp screen.

# Shop hours and morning preparation

Set the shop's opening days, opening time, closing time and IANA timezone in Settings → Shop details → Shop hours. No hours are invented or seeded by migration 53. A closing time earlier than opening time means that shift ends the following day. Weekday selection refers to the day the shift opens. Open/closed status follows this saved weekly schedule, independently of the bot's reply-pause switch; this version does not model holiday exceptions or an early-close override.

In the connected owner AI chat, say “Open the shop”, “Start my day”, or “கடை திறக்கலாம்”. The existing `owner_daily_briefing` tool uses the saved timezone and last scheduled closing boundary, includes yesterday's orders, orders since closing, current order/inventory checks and current bot-health counts, and returns a preparation checklist. The connected AI must process pending review feedback, page through yesterday/today's conversation audit, investigate existing inventory, save supported match/change cards, and inspect operations/customer-help evidence before reporting completion. A short report should show yesterday, overnight requests, inventory priorities, customer issues, and review cards, with at most three priorities. The host AI controls the subsequent tool calls; the briefing server does not invoke a model or force another client to run. Missing hours cause a Settings setup note, not a routine closing-time question.

Closed-shop notes are attached to the final caption of draft/submitted customer bills and owner-confirmed price bills. A draft says it can be sent; a submitted order says the request is noted. Neither claims pickup is ready. Direct opening-hours questions use the same schedule. Schedule evaluation occurs when a reply is generated. Nothing broadcasts to customers merely because time passes.

Orders show the saved WhatsApp number with a `tel:` link. Clicking opens the device's dialer or configured calling application; it does not place an automatic call. Simulator identities and invalid numbers are not dialable.

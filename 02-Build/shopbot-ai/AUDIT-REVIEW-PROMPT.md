# A more useful ChatGPT catalog review

Use this prompt after refreshing the inventory app tools. It asks for a practical conversation and real catalog options before decisions or edits.

> Review the last 72 hours of customer conversations for product-matching problems. Page all available audit evidence. Start a new audit if the period differs from an existing audit; keep the earlier audit unchanged. For each important unresolved customer term, search the real catalog using several likely English, Tamil and Tanglish names and product-family terms, not only the original words. Show up to three plausible real products with their names, photos if available, prices, and a short note about what matches or differs. Do not present a different material, size, or product type as exact. If you cannot find an appropriate product, say so. Give me a short numbered list and ask one grouped question about the choices or aliases you need me to confirm. Mention bot bugs briefly and keep their details available on request. Save findings with their evidence, but do not change product aliases until I authorize the selected changes.

Example follow-up after looking at candidates:

> For the reviewed products I confirmed, add the selected search names in one batch. Leave all uncertain choices, speech-recognition issues and unrelated products alone. Preview the exact changes first, and report success or conflicts.

The client may ask for write permission. A candidate is not confirmation of the customer's intention, a material specification, or stock. A host application might not support displaying images from MCP responses; in that case the agent can show names, prices and available links without claiming photos were displayed.
